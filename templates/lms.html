Update Rule

w <- w + (mu / (x.x + eps)) e x with e = d - y. The filter nudges weights toward the clean target on every sample.

What To Listen For

Try the same clip with small and large step sizes. Stable settings usually clean up the signal more smoothly, while aggressive ones can sound brittle or unstable.

What To Watch

The noisy waveform stays jagged, the ADALINE output tries to follow the clean target, and the smoothed squared error should trend downward.
